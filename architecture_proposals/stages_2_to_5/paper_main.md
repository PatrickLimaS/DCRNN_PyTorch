# Probingnoise: Graph-Organized Local-Global Adaptation under Temporal Shift

## Abstract

Probingnoise is a scoped methods project on graph-organized local-global adaptation under temporal shift in tasks with shared-signal structure across nodes per timestep. The currently supported load-bearing mechanism is the combination of skip-connected graph coordination and skip-connected local-global aggregation. Residual gating and adaptive scheduling remain conditional extensions. All empirical evidence to date comes from two synthetic stand-ins; no real-benchmark result is yet claimed. Wild-Time Yearbook is treated as a scope/falsification probe rather than as the strongest positive-fit validation benchmark.
