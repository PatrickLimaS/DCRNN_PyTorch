# Final Main Paragraph

This work introduces an architectural extension layered on top of the existing Probingnoise core rather than a replacement for it. The base architecture — a local temporal encoder, a graph-coordination block with residual skip and layer normalization, a local–global aggregation block with residual skip, and a task head, with residual gating and adaptive scheduling retained as optional extensions — remains the implemented synchronous, Colab-trainable core, and all empirical results reported in this paper are produced by that implementation under synchronous execution. We situate this core within the broader asynchronous-computation lineage (Bertsekas and Tsitsiklis, 1989; Niu et al., 2011; Faber and Wattenhofer, 2022, 2024) only as a principled direction for future systems-level development; no property of that lineage is claimed for the present implementation. On top of this core, we specify a visual-evidence extension for multimodal prediction composed of a visual front-end, a visual representation encoder, a bridge module that projects visual representations into the Probingnoise stack, and a prediction head operating over the fused multimodal representation produced by that bridge. The extension is additive: it consumes the outputs of the existing core without modifying its internal blocks, residual pathways, or training regime. Its component choices draw on established work on transformer-based image representation, vision–language pretraining, frozen-encoder bridging, and multimodal chain-of-thought prompting (Dosovitskiy et al., 2021; Radford et al., 2021; Li et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023/2024), and no property demonstrated in those works is claimed as a property of the present system. Novelty is claimed at the level of integration — specifically, how visual representations are introduced into the Probingnoise residual structure while preserving its training regime — rather than at the level of the constituent visual or multimodal components. The contribution of the present paper is therefore deliberately narrow and architectural, with empirical validation of the multimodal extension deferred to future work.
