# ACL/IEEE Short Paragraph

We extend the Probingnoise architecture by layering a new module on top of its existing core rather than replacing that core. The implemented base — local temporal encoder, graph-coordination block with residual skip and LayerNorm, local–global aggregation with residual skip, and task head, with optional residual gating and adaptive scheduling — remains synchronous and Colab-trainable, and is the system producing all empirical results reported here. We situate it within the asynchronous-computation lineage (Bertsekas and Tsitsiklis, 1989; Niu et al., 2011; Faber and Wattenhofer, 2022, 2024) as a future-systems direction only, without claiming asynchronous operation of the present implementation. On top of the core we specify an additive visual-evidence extension for multimodal prediction, comprising a visual front-end, a visual representation encoder, a bridge module into the Probingnoise stack, and a prediction head over the fused representation. Component choices follow established multimodal work (Dosovitskiy et al., 2021; Radford et al., 2021; Li et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023/2024); novelty is claimed only at the level of integration into the existing residual structure. The contribution is architecture-level; empirical validation of the extension is deferred to future work.
