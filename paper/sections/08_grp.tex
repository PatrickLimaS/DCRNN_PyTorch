\section{Global Re-entry Projection (GRP)}
\label{sec:grp}

GRP is a proposed architectural operator for Stage~B of the cascade
(Section~\ref{sec:cascade-abc}). It is specified in this paper; it is not
empirically validated.

\subsection{Specification}
\begin{align}
  g_t &= P(H_t) \\
  p_{i,t} &= W_g \, g_t \\
  \tilde{h}_{i,t} &= \mathrm{LN}(h_{i,t} + \alpha \, p_{i,t})
\end{align}
Setting $\alpha = 0$ recovers the unmodified core.

\subsection{Claim boundary}
No empirical claim; validation is gate-blocked by the baseline reproduction step.
