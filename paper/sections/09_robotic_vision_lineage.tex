\section{Robotic Vision Lineage}
\label{sec:robotic-vision-lineage}

Robotic vision systems face the three asynchronous regimes of the
cascade (Section~\ref{sec:cascade-abc}): multi-rate sensing,
heterogeneous compute, anytime decision emission. The correspondence
is architectural; no robotics task is implemented here.

Lineage: event vision~\citep{gallego2022eventvision}; VLA
models~\citep{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,octo2024}; robot data at
scale~\citep{khazatsky2024droid}. All cited as lineage only.
