# Related Lineages (Structure and Concept)

## Purpose

Every research lineage intersecting this project at architectural structure (asynchrony, multi-rate, residual composition, event-driven) or conceptual target (ruptura de regularidade, burst detection, regime change, anytime inference). Lineage only — no capability inherited.

## Structural lineages

- **Async computation:** Bertsekas and Tsitsiklis (1989); Niu et al. (2011).
- **Async graph learning:** Faber and Wattenhofer (2022, 2024).
- **Event-based / neuromorphic vision:** Gallego et al. (2022); Schaefer et al. (2022).
- **State-space streaming:** Gu et al. (2022) S4; Gu and Dao (2023) Mamba.
- **Anytime / early-exit:** Teerapittayanon et al. (2016); Huang et al. (2018).
- **VLA / multimodal:** Dosovitskiy et al. (2021); Radford et al. (2021); Li et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2024); Brohan et al. (2023); Kim et al. (2024); Octo Model Team (2024); Khazatsky et al. (2024).

## Conceptual lineages

- **Change-point detection:** Adams and MacKay (2007); Aminikhanghahi and Cook (2017).
- **Anomaly detection:** Blázquez-García et al. (2021); Ma et al. (2021).
- **Concept drift:** Gama et al. (2014); Lu et al. (2019).
- **Burst / neural dynamics:** Legenstein and Maass (2011); Shew and Plenz (2013).
- **Spiking systems:** Maass (1997); Tavanaei et al. (2019).
