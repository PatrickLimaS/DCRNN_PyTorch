\section{Cognitive Stack --- Draft Specification}
\label{sec:cognitive-stack-draft}

\noindent\textbf{Status: Draft.} Items flagged \textsc{Pending} are required
before any operational claim.

\subsection{Position}

Proposed as a structure parallel to the cascade
(\S\ref{sec:cascade-abc}), not a replacement.

\subsection{Five provisional layers}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule

\textsc{Pending}: input/output signatures, internal state, update rule,
failure mode for each layer.

\subsection{Internal dialogue layer}

Transversal element monitoring $L_1$--$L_5$ with predefined schedule.
\textsc{Pending}: monitored signal, emitted modulation form, schedule type.

\subsection{Interface candidates}

Three framings enumerated, none adopted: (A)~stack as consumer;
(B)~stack as co-processor; (C)~stack embedded in Stage~B (rejected).

\subsection{Claim boundary}

Nothing implemented. ``Under specification'' in the Evidence Matrix
corresponds to this document.
