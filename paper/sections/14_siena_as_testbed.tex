\section{Siena as a Multi-Channel Temporal Testbed}
\label{sec:siena-testbed}

The Siena Scalp EEG Database~\citep{siena2020physionet} is used as a
testbed for the cascade's multi-channel temporal regime, not as a
seizure-detection benchmark.

\subsection{Why Siena fits the stack}

Siena provides 29--35 scalp EEG channels per recording. Stage~A's
multi-rate ingestion specification is exercised against a graph of
sensors arranged by the 10-20 system. Seizure onset is, by clinical
definition, a rupture of temporal regularity. The electrode arrangement
forms a spatial graph by construction, fitting the DCRNN substrate.

\subsection{Scope of claims}

Once results are produced, the testbed authorises statements that the
cascade ingests real multi-channel temporal data and that the recurrent
core can be applied to EEG input. It does not authorise comparison
against published seizure-detection baselines or transfer from the
cognitive stack's proposed layers (\S\ref{sec:cognitive-stack-draft}).

\subsection{Relation to the baseline gate}

The \texttt{dcrnn\_baseline} METR-LA reproduction gate remains
independent of Siena. Both tracks proceed in parallel; neither alone
authorises capability claims.
